Top half of the screen

These commands go on the control line. ESQ reads them only when its display is not busy.

Sends a clear each 150 seconds. The logo rotation of ESQ stays stopped, and LOGO.LST does not change.

Promo

ESQ finds the program in its listings. A part of the title is enough. Put the title in quotes to find only that title.

Local ads

One ad on each line. ESQ shows 6 lines of 40 characters. Tokens: %CENTER%, %LEFT%, %RIGHT%, %COLOR%%BLACK%%CYAN% and the other colors. Sending the ads replaces all the ads of ESQ.

weather.gov

Gets the current weather and the 3-day forecast of a US ZIP code from weather.gov, and sends them. The label and the status text of the weather form are not used.

Weather

Sends the weather on the data line, and then shows it in the top half.

3-day forecast

Sends a picture, a high and a low for the next 3 days, and then shows them in the top half. It also sends the label and the status text of the weather form. An empty field shows "???".

Data line

Send the clock, or read the guide again and send the changes.

Playbook commands

Any playbook commands in YAML. Prevue Commander sends them as they are written, so put data line commands in a session (!!address, !!configuration, ..., !!boxOff).
